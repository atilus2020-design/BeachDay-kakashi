Resources

These are the official sources Beach Day relies on. For beach safety, use the NWS links and the lifeguard flags. Beach Day is a planning aid, not a safety service.

  • NWS Miami Surf Zone Forecast: rip current risk for South Florida.
  • NWS beach forecasts for Florida: map of rip current risk by area.
  • NWS rip current safety: what a rip current is and how to get out of one.

  • NWS heat index: feels-like temperature categories.
